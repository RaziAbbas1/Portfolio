AVAILABILITY

HA, DRS and vMotion

HA restarts VMs when a host fails. DRS balances load across hosts. vMotion moves a running VM with no downtime. Together they make patching and failures routine.

PROTECTION

Snapshots are not backups

A snapshot depends on its base disk and lives on the same datastore. It is a short-term safety net before a change, not a recovery plan.

HCI

vSAN and Nutanix AOS

Hyperconverged infrastructure pools the local disks of every node into shared storage. Data is copied across nodes (FTT on vSAN, RF2 or RF3 on Nutanix), so a node failure does not lose data.

UNDER THE HOOD

Hardware still matters

Disk, PSU, fan and memory faults show up as hypervisor alerts first. Reading iDRAC or iLO logs, firmware baselines and vendor cases is part of keeping clusters healthy.

PLAYBOOK

My first five minutes on a P1.

A calm routine beats heroics. This is how I work an outage, on VMware, Nutanix or Windows.

Scope it

What is down, who is affected, since when, and is it one host or the whole cluster?

What changed?

Recent patches, migrations, network or firmware changes are the usual suspects.

Read the logs

vmkernel and hostd on ESXi, NCC and genesis on Nutanix, event logs on Windows, hardware logs in iDRAC or iLO.

Stabilize

Fail over, vMotion away, roll back or restart the right service. Service first, theory later.

Write the RCA

Root cause, timeline, fix and prevention, so the same page never fires twice.
